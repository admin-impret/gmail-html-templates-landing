Privacy policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

What the extension does

HTML Templates for Gmail lets you save reusable HTML email templates and insert them into Gmail's compose window.

What data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or email content.

Where your data lives

Your templates are stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device. They never leave your browser. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

Permissions

  • storage - to save your templates on your device.
  • mail.google.com access - to show the Templates button inside Gmail compose. The extension does not read your emails; it only inserts the template you choose into the draft you are writing.

Third parties

The extension uses no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no external servers.
