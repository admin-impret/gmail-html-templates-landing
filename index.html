The problem

Manage reusable raw HTML outside Gmail's built-in template workflow. If you send the same kind of email often - invoices, client updates, follow-ups, newsletters - you either retype it, dig up an old thread, or watch pasted HTML lose its formatting. This extension fixes that in the place you already write email.

What you get

One click in compose

A Templates button sits next to Send. Pick a template and it's inserted at your cursor, with supported HTML formatting.

Save HTML templates

Paste HTML email code from your editor or designer, preview it, save it and reuse it. Unsupported HTML may be removed for safety.

Starter library

Ready-made templates for follow-ups, invoice reminders, weekly client updates and thank-you notes.

Private by design

Templates live in your browser's local storage. No extension account or template-hosting server. Inserting a template copies it into your Gmail draft.

How it works

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the template manager and paste your HTML - or start from a ready-made one.
  3. In Gmail compose, click Templates and choose. Done.
