The problem

Gmail can't store rich templates. If you send the same kind of email often - invoices, client updates, follow-ups, newsletters - you either retype it, dig up an old thread, or watch pasted HTML lose its formatting. This extension fixes that in the place you already write email.

What you get

One click in compose

A Templates button sits next to Send. Pick a template and it's inserted at your cursor, formatting intact.

Paste any HTML

Design in Mailchimp, Figma, Canva or anywhere else. Paste the code, save it, reuse it forever.

Starter library

Ready-made templates for follow-ups, invoice reminders, weekly client updates and thank-you notes.

Private by design

Templates live in your browser's local storage. No account, no sign-up, nothing uploaded anywhere.

How it works

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the template manager and paste your HTML - or start from a ready-made one.
  3. In Gmail compose, click Templates and choose. Done.
